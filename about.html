02 / Who it’s for And what it can’t do yet

Who it’s for.

People with dyslexia

Talk instead of type, and get squiggles that wait until you’ve finished the word. Built by someone who lives with it.

Writing in a second language

Fix spelling without worrying that an AI has quietly changed what you meant. Every change is shown word by word.

Work where details matter

Finance, law, health, engineering: a dropped “not” or a changed number is a real problem. Repeit refuses those edits.

What Repeit catches today
  • Spelling, including many phonetic misspellings
  • Punctuation and capitals
  • Common grammar slips
  • Edits that would change your meaning
Not yet
  • Languages other than English
  • Every article (a/the) and word-choice error
  • Every real-word mix-up like their/there in context
  • Strong accents: accuracy can drop, and we’re testing

04 / Under the hood No magic. Here’s what runs.

Under the hood.

Speech

OpenAI Whisper

Runs on your device. Choose Fast, Balanced or Best accuracy (tiny / base / small models).

Writing help

Qwen3-4B

A 4-billion-parameter model, running on your device. Optional: bring your own Claude or ChatGPT key.

Languages

English, for now

Dictation and checking are English today (US, UK and more spellings). More languages are on the way.

Works on

macOS 13 or later

Apple silicon and Intel Macs. Windows, iPhone and Android are coming.

Download

About 0.5–2.5 GB

One-time model download on first launch. After that, it works offline.
