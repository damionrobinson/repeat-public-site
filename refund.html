The short version. If Repeit isn’t right for you, you can have a full refund within 14 days of buying, for any reason.

14-day refund

You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of buying a Yearly or Pay once licence, for any reason. You don’t have to explain why.

How to ask

  • Email support@repeit.com, or
  • use the link in your Paddle receipt.

Licences are sold by our reseller, Paddle.com, the merchant of record, so the refund comes from Paddle to the way you paid.

Your other rights

This doesn’t affect your other legal rights as a consumer. The full terms say more about buying a licence.
