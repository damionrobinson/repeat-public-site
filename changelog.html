Changelog.

Everything we’ve changed, newest first. If it touches what Repeit sends anywhere, we say so in bold.

Beta · 12 November 2026
  • The pill says “Cancelled” when you press Esc, instead of just vanishing.
  • Menu bar status: “Repeit is on” or “Repeit is paused”.
  • A quiet mic gets its own mic-off icon, not a generic warning.
  • “Dismiss” is now “Keep mine” on suggestion cards.
  • Licence screen rebuilt: trial, licensed, expired (with Renew) and more.
  • Removed an old daily limit on rewrites. There is no daily cap.
  • Lifetime licences are supported.
Beta · 11 November 2026
  • Esc cancels a dictation and throws it away.
  • A quick accidental tap no longer starts hands-free dictation.
  • Warning when your mic has been too quiet for a while.
  • Pause from the menu bar: 1 hour, until tomorrow, or in this app.
  • Never checks To/Cc/Bcc or search boxes. New Block list in Settings.
  • Squiggles wait until you’ve written three words.
  • Report a problem from the menu bar.
  • Settings → Licence: activate, deactivate, works offline.
Beta · 10 November 2026
  • Hold or double-tap Right Command to dictate.
  • Double-tap ⌥ to accept a suggestion.
  • Dictation stops hearing “licence” as “life insurance”.
  • When Repeit has to use the clipboard, it tells you why.
