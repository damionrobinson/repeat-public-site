Opening Repeit

The beta isn't notarised by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it the first time you open it. That's normal for a beta, and it only takes a minute to get past.

macOS will say it "can't check Repeit for malware". That's expected until Apple signing is done, not a sign anything's wrong with the download.

What you need

A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 13 or newer, and about 4 GB of free disk space. On first launch Repeit downloads its on-device models once; after that it works offline.

1. Open the download and drag Repeit to Applications

Double-click the downloaded .dmg file (for example Repeit-0.3.0-build6.dmg). In the window that opens, drag Repeit onto the Applications folder next to it. The window also has a short "How to open Repeit" note with these same steps.

2. Open it the first time

Open Applications and double-click Repeit. macOS shows a warning and won't open it yet. Instead:

  1. Click Done (not Move to Bin).
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Repeit.
  3. Confirm with your password or Touch ID. You only do this once for each download.

Prefer Terminal? xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Repeit.app does the same.

3. Set up

  • Repeit downloads what it needs first (usually under a minute) and shows its progress. The larger writing model carries on in the background.
  • Allow Microphone (for dictation) and Accessibility (to see and fix text where you type) when Repeit asks.
  • Your 30-day free trial starts by itself. No card, no key. Dictation and transcription are free anyway.

4. Closing and quitting

Closing the window, or pressing ⌘Q, puts Repeit away in the menu bar, where it keeps working. To quit it for real, click its menu bar icon and choose Quit Repeit.

5. Updates

There's no auto-update for the beta yet. Check back here for a newer download and repeat steps 1 and 2 for it.
