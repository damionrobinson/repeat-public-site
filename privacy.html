Privacy notice

Last updated: 12 November 2026

The short version. Repeat works on your device. What you write and what you say never leave it unless you choose to send text to an AI provider you connect yourself. We don’t have accounts, we don’t sell data, and we don’t show ads.

On this website we use Google Analytics only if you accept it. When you buy, Paddle handles your payment. Below is everything, in plain English.

1. Who we are

Design Definition Limited, a company registered in the United Kingdom, makes Repeat and runs repeit.com. We are the “controller” of the personal data described here, except where we say someone else is. Company number and registered address to be added. Contact: support@repeit.com.

2. The Repeat app

What stays on your device (we never receive it)

  • Everything you dictate, type, select, proofread or rewrite, and the audio of your voice.
  • Your history, notes, dictionary, snippets, style rules, meeting recordings and transcripts.
  • API keys for any AI provider you connect. They are stored in your device’s secure keychain.

Speech recognition, checking and rewriting run on your device and work offline once set up. Repeat never reads password or other secure fields.

What leaves your device, and why

  • Model downloads. On first use, Repeat downloads its speech and writing model files from Hugging Face. Like any download, Hugging Face sees your IP address. No personal content is sent.
  • Free trial and licence. Starting a trial or activating a licence sends your licence key (if you have one) and an anonymous device code to our licence server at licence.repeit.com. This lets us enforce the 3-device limit. No name, email, hardware serial number or content is sent. After activation, Repeat works offline and checks in only occasionally.
  • “Help us improve Repeat” (off unless you turn it on). If you switch it on, Repeat sends anonymous counts, such as “dictation used”, “a fix was accepted” or “the app started”, and crash reports (where in the code a crash happened, never what you were writing). If you have activated a licence, your licence key is attached so we can fix problems affecting it. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.
  • Feedback you choose to send. When you use “Report a problem” or send feedback, we receive your message, any screenshot you attach, and app details (app version, operating system) unless you untick them.
  • AI providers you connect (optional). If you connect Anthropic, OpenAI or your own server, Repeat sends the text you ask it to rewrite or check straight to that provider, only when you ask. We don’t see it. That provider’s privacy policy applies.

3. This website

Analytics and cookies

When you first visit, we ask whether you accept analytics. If you say no, or don’t answer, we don’t load Google Analytics and no analytics cookies are set. If you accept, we use Google Analytics 4 to learn how many people visit, which pages they look at, roughly where they are (country and city, worked out from the IP address, which Google Analytics 4 doesn’t store), what device and browser they use, how they found us, and whether they sign up for the beta or buy. We have switched off Google signals, ad personalisation and sharing our data with Google for its own purposes. Google acts as our processor. We keep this data for 2 months.

Beta sign-up

If you join the beta, we collect your email address and any optional answers you give (what you mostly write, and whether you’re interested in the accessibility price or translation help). We use them to send you the beta and news about Repeat, and to understand who it’s for. Our form provider, Formspree, processes the sign-up for us. We keep your details until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, and in any case no more than 12 months after the beta ends. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Buying a licence

Paddle.com is the merchant of record. It collects your name, email, payment and billing details, and is responsible for that data under Paddle’s privacy policy. Paddle tells us about the sale: a transaction ID, the product, the country, and your email address. We use your email address once, to send your licence key through our email provider, Resend. We don’t store it in our licence system. We keep the licence key, transaction ID and the anonymous device codes that activated it while the licence is valid, plus 6 years for tax and accounting records.

Emails to us

If you email support@repeit.com, we keep the conversation for as long as we need it to help you, and then no more than 2 years.

Hosting and fonts

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and our licence server runs on DigitalOcean. Like all web servers, they briefly log IP addresses for security. The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser asks Google for the font files and Google sees your IP address.

4. Our legal reasons

  • Consent: Google Analytics, the optional “Help us improve Repeat” sharing, and beta emails. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Contract: licences, trials, device activation and sending your licence key.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping our servers secure, preventing licence abuse and copying, answering feedback and support emails, and enforcing our terms.
  • Legal obligation: tax and accounting records.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or export your personal data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent. Email support@repeit.com; we reply within one month. Because Repeat keeps your content on your device, you control it directly: deleting Repeat or its data folder deletes it. If you’re unhappy, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk), or to your local data protection authority if you live in the EU.

7. Children

Repeat and this website aren’t aimed at children under 13, and the beta sign-up is for people aged 16 and over. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we’ll delete it.

8. Security

We keep what we hold to a minimum, use encrypted connections, sign licence data so it can’t be forged, and store API keys and licences in the operating system’s secure keychain on your device.

9. Changes

If we change this notice, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change matters, for example a new kind of data, we’ll say so on this site and, for licence holders, by email, before it applies.
