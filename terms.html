Usage information

By default, Repeat shares anonymous counts of how you use the app — for example, that dictation was used, or a spelling fix was applied — to help us improve it. This never includes anything you've dictated, written, or had checked; the app is built so that information structurally can't be included, not just kept out by policy. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.

Feedback

If you send us feedback through the app, that message is sent directly to us when you choose to send it. We never see it before then.

Free trial

Clicking “Try Personal, 14 days free” registers this device with our server, to track your trial. This never includes anything you've dictated, written, or had checked. Dictation, file transcription, basic cleanup, and your everyday free use of proofreading and rewrites are never affected by this and never involve a server call — this only happens if and when you choose to start a Personal trial.
