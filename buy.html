FAQ / Before you buy Short answers

Good questions.

What’s free, for good?

Dictation, transcription, underlines and autocorrect. No trial clock on those.

What happens after a year on Yearly?

Everything you already have keeps working. Renew to keep the writing help and new updates.

What does Pay once include?

Keep Repeit with its writing help for good. You get every update and new feature for 5 years. After that, everything you have keeps working, and you can renew updates if you want the newest version.

Can I reinstall or switch devices?

Reinstalling on the same device never uses up a slot. One key covers 3 different devices.

Does my writing go to your servers?

No. Words and voice are processed on your device. We only see counts like words dictated, and you can turn that off.
